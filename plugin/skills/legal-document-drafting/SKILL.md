# legal-document-drafting

## Scope and activation
Draft Vietnamese legal documents from verified requirements and supplied facts.
Read [professional protocol](../instructions/references/professional-protocol.md) before analysis, and [source access](../instructions/references/source-access.md) when sources must be retrieved. Apply these even on direct invocation.

## Inputs
Document type, recipient, purpose, parties, facts, dates, procedural stage, source template and execution requirements.
Record missing material inputs; do not invent them.

## Workflow
1. Confirm intended legal function and required form, competence, filing channel and timing through verified sources.
2. Map facts to required sections; use clearly marked fields for missing facts, never invent names, signatures or dates.
3. Draft consistent definitions, rights, obligations, remedies and execution provisions appropriate to the document.
4. Check factual assertions, annexes, cross-references, authority and supporting documents.
5. Provide a clean draft, unresolved-field list and signing/filing checklist; audit before delivery.

## Professional checklist
- Correct document and authority
- Mandatory content and attachments
- Unfilled fields visible
- No fabricated certifications or signatures

## Legal authority checks
Use the shared source and claim ledgers. Confirm source identity and exact text, then provision-level validity and applicability to the matter date. For this module, explicitly verify: correct document and authority and unfilled fields visible. If unavailable, label the affected conclusion unverified. Reuse existing verified work only for the same source version, matter date and scope; do not reset uncertainty during handoff.

## Quality criteria
Draft can be traced to facts and instructions; unsupported legal bases are omitted or marked for verification.
Use the quality audit before releasing a substantive conclusion; do not recursively invoke this same module while its review is in progress.

## Output
Draft headed DỰ THẢO; assumptions; fields requiring completion; legal basis register; execution checklist.

## Limits and handoff
Contested arguments go to legal-reasoning; negotiated contracts go to vietnam-contract-review; no filing, service or execution without separate authorization.
Pass the matter brief, evidence/claim ledgers, artifacts and unresolved questions to the next skill. Route by exact sibling skill name in [router](../instructions/SKILL.md); load its SKILL.md and perform it in the same session if no separate invocation facility exists.
